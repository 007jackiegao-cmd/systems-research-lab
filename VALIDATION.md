## Meaning of the evidence

A test suite establishes the behaviour of the named calculations and cases. Browser checks establish the inspected interactions. File readback establishes the structure of a tested export. None of these alone establishes full deployment, external acceptance, production security, physical suitability or commercial performance.

No unverified latency target, real financial result, private research capability or personal achievement is presented as a portfolio outcome.
